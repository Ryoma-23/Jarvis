# Jarvis visual design system

## Principle

The Neural Core is the scene. Header, status, System Log, Conversation, and
controls are quiet instruments floating over it. Visual hierarchy comes from
light, depth, motion direction, and typography—not heavy frames or dashboard
cards. Persistent effects remain low contrast so the interface is comfortable
during long sessions; strong energy is reserved for short, meaningful events.

## Color roles

| Color | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Cyan | Idle, Listening, healthy connection, primary interaction |
| Violet | Thinking and AI processing |
| Mint | Speaking and successful completion |
| Amber | Connecting and recoverable attention |
| Red | Error only |
| White | Very small nucleus peak and primary readable text |

The background remains blue-black (`#03070d` through `#071827`). Bloom must
preserve hue and saturation; increasing energy must not turn broad areas white.

## Motion language

- Inward motion means input, connection, or concentration.
- Outward motion means Jarvis output or a completed stability release.
- Directional inner flow means Tool activity.
- A single irregular impulse means Error.
- Slow convection and breathing mean Idle readiness.

State profiles damp over 0.4–1.2 seconds. Persistent blinking, detached rings,
mouse-following parallax, and large repeating flashes are intentionally absent.

## Layer hierarchy

1. Blue-black atmospheric background
2. Sparse spatial particles and perspective floor
3. Volumetric Aura and Shader Core
4. Core state and Tool readouts
5. Edge overlays for Log and Conversation
6. Header, voice controls, notification, and Footer

At narrow widths, information is reduced before the Core is displaced: System
Log hides at 900px and Conversation moves to the bottom at 680px.
